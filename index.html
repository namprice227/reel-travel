Before you pack

A few good
questions.

Can I start with places I already know?

Yes. Add your places and trip preferences, then generate a plan from your selected places. You don’t need a folder full of saved videos to get started.

Does every social link work automatically?

No. Some sources aren’t accessible. You can add the place name or caption, paste notes, or upload a screenshot to keep planning. A saved link stays attached to its inspiration.

Can I change the itinerary?

Yes. Move, add, replace or remove flexible stops. Regenerating rebuilds the trip from your current places and preferences; fixed bookings keep their times.

Does Routelet book anything for me?

No. It helps you plan. Suggested meals and activities are not reservations. Check current opening hours, travel routes and availability, then book directly with the venue.
